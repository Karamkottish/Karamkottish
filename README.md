    D["🔍 Discover<br/>user pain"] --> R["🎯 Define<br/>roadmap & KPIs"]

    M -.feedback loop.-> D

1. **Start with the problem, not the tech.** Every sprint maps to a measurable business outcome.
2. **Small, frequent releases.** CI/CD and feature flags over big-bang launches.
3. **Teams own outcomes.** As a PM I gave each team (AI, Backend, Frontend, Flutter, Marketing) clear goals and room to decide how.
4. **Performance is a feature.** 60fps animations and fast load times are part of the spec, not a nice-to-have.
